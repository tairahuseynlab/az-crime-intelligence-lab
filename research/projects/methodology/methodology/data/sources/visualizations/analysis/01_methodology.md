# Methodology

## Objective

This research examines long-term patterns in recorded crime in Azerbaijan using publicly available official statistics.

## Research Approach

The analysis follows a basic criminal intelligence research workflow:

1. Define the research question
2. Identify reliable open sources
3. Collect relevant data
4. Evaluate source quality
5. Analyze temporal and population-adjusted patterns
6. Identify notable changes requiring further investigation
7. Document findings and limitations

## Indicators

Two primary indicators are used:

- Total number of recorded crimes
- Recorded crimes per 100,000 population

## Analytical Principles

The analysis distinguishes between:

- Raw crime counts
- Population-adjusted rates
- Observed statistical patterns
- Possible explanations

Observed changes are not treated as proof of causation.

## Limitations

Recorded crime statistics may be affected by reporting practices, law-enforcement activity, legislation, population changes, and other factors.

Therefore, the findings should be interpreted as an analysis of recorded crime data rather than a complete measurement of all criminal activity.

## Data Source

State Statistical Committee of the Republic of Azerbaijan, *Offences in Azerbaijan*.
