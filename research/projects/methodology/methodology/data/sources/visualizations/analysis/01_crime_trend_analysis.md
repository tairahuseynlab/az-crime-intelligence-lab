# Crime Trend Analysis: Azerbaijan, 1993–2024

## Research Question

How did recorded crime levels and population-adjusted crime rates in Azerbaijan change between 1993 and 2024?

## Data Source

The data are based on the State Statistical Committee of the Republic of Azerbaijan publication "Offences in Azerbaijan".

## Indicators

This analysis uses two indicators:

- Total number of recorded crimes
- Recorded crimes per 100,000 population

## Key Findings

Recorded crime reached its lowest level in the dataset in 2000, with 13,958 recorded crimes.

The number of recorded crimes subsequently increased over the following years, reaching 36,811 in 2023.

In 2024, recorded crime decreased to 33,379.

The population-adjusted crime rate followed a broadly similar pattern. It was 173 per 100,000 population in 2000 and reached 363 in 2023, before decreasing to 327 in 2024.

## Important Observation

The increase in recorded crime does not, by itself, prove that underlying criminal activity increased by the same amount.

Recorded statistics can be influenced by reporting practices, law-enforcement activity, legislation, population changes, and other factors.

## 2020–2024

A notable change occurred during 2021–2023:

- 2020: 26,004 recorded crimes
- 2021: 31,131
- 2022: 36,494
- 2023: 36,811
- 2024: 33,379

This period requires further analysis by crime category and geographic distribution.

## Limitations

This dataset represents recorded crime and does not measure crimes that were not reported or detected.

The data also cannot establish causal explanations for observed changes.
