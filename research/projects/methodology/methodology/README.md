# Methodology

This section documents the analytical methods, frameworks, concepts, and research approaches studied and developed by the AZ-Crime Intelligence Lab initiative.

## Areas of Methodological Development

- Criminal Intelligence Analysis
- Crime Pattern and Trend Analysis
- Temporal Analysis
- Geographic Analysis
- Link Analysis
- Risk and Threat Assessment
- Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)
- Data Analysis and Visualization
- Criminal Behaviour Analysis

## Approach

The initiative studies internationally recognized analytical approaches and evaluates their potential relevance to the Azerbaijani context.

Methods will be documented transparently, with attention to evidence, limitations, ethics, privacy, and responsible use of information.

## Development Process

1. Study established international methodologies.
2. Review academic and institutional literature.
3. Identify relevant analytical principles.
4. Evaluate applicability to the Azerbaijani context.
5. Develop and test research frameworks using appropriate public data.
6. Document findings, limitations, and potential improvements.
